# Electra Therapeutics

> Clinical-stage biotechnology company in South San Francisco, California, developing first-in-class antibody therapies against signal regulatory proteins (SIRP) for immunological diseases and cancer. Lead candidate ELA026 (ipsoprubart) targets SIRP-alpha/beta1/gamma to deplete the pathological myeloid cells and T lymphocytes that drive secondary hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis (sHLH), and is in the pivotal SURPASS Phase 2/3 study. A second program, ELA822, targets activated T lymphocytes for immunology and inflammation.
